# Mizen financing knowledge audit — 2026-10-10

Scope: source-by-source review of selected Tunisian financing mechanisms. This is an evidence review, not a legal opinion or a guarantee of provider acceptance. “Published maximum” means an advertised ceiling subject to the programme’s rules; it is not an entitlement.

## Verified against official provider pages

## Stacking evidence rules

1. A source saying a guarantor covers eligible loans or leasing does **not** mean two independent financing products can always be stacked on one project.
2. A grant, subsidy, repayable endowment, equity contribution, loan and guarantee are distinct funding roles. Never add guarantee coverage to cash funding.
3. “No direct compatibility claim found” means UNKNOWN, not incompatible. “Potentially compatible” is not approval.
4. Do not infer that an applicant can combine BTS Crédit sur l’honneur with BTS Crédit Professionnel, ANETI support, FOPRODI, SICAR equity or leasing unless the applicable rule/convention and provider confirm it.
5. Keep historical legal instruments and older coverage schedules queryable for audit, but do not use them as current operating terms without confirmation.

The pages above establish what the providers publicly state; they do not independently establish current acceptance of every application, all fee variations, or every compatibility combination. Detailed legal eligibility for Crédit sur l’honneur remains open until the decree and BCT circular are read directly and reconciled to BTS's implementation process.
